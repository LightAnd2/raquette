Raquette

Privacy and terms

Last updated October 2026

Raquette is a free demo that analyses tennis rally videos. This page explains what happens to what you upload, in plain language.

What we collect

  • The video you upload.
  • The player name you type, if any, and whether you picked right- or left-handed.
  • The analysis results made from your video.

There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or tracking.

Where it goes and how long we keep it

Videos are processed on a server hosted by Hugging Face. The website itself is hosted by Vercel. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the page loads.

Uploaded videos and their results are deleted automatically 24 hours after upload. Until then, anyone with the results link can view them. The link contains a long random ID, so it can't be guessed, but don't share it if you want the results kept private.

What you agree to

  • Only upload footage you have the right to use.
  • Don't upload anything illegal, harmful, or private footage of people who haven't agreed to it.
  • Raquette is provided as is, for fun and practice. Results are automated estimates and can be wrong, so don't rely on them for officiating, betting or anything important.
  • The demo can be slow, change, or go offline at any time.

Removal requests and questions

To have something removed sooner, or for any question, contact the developer through GitHub.
